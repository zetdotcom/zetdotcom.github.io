Follow two requests through a race

A person searches for oak (request A), then elm (request B). The field stays on elm. Predict what the screen will display when both responses have arrived, then step through the trace to check.

    Look for the first wrong write

    In the B-then-A run, notice the moment when the screen is correct. Advance once more. Which response changes it? Restart with the guard enabled. That same response still arrives, but you can now see why the screen keeps its previous value.

    Try A-then-B as well. A correct final screen can hide an earlier flash of the wrong results or a spinner that disappeared too soon. Compare the state immediately after the third event in each run.
