Practice a few everyday Vim moves

Try a few Vim movements on a single line of JavaScript. Choose a task, look at the marked cursor, and predict where a command will take you. Use the command buttons with a pointer or with Tab and Enter.

Command reference

w moves to the next word start, b to a previous word start, and e to a word end. Punctuation can be a stop of its own.

% moves between the parentheses. In Normal mode, vi( starts a visual selection inside the pair, leaving the delimiters outside. Esc returns to Normal mode.

Take it back to your editor

Find a call with two arguments. Try vi( and check exactly what was selected. Press Escape, then try ci( in your editor to replace the arguments. Finish by navigating to the definition of the function and returning to the call site.
